Sustainability

The Ages

Explore Intel’s journey through innovation, sustainability, and the future of technology.

1968

Intel Founded

Intel began as a visionary semiconductor startup, inventing the foundation of modern computing and building a future-driven legacy.

1971

First Microprocessor

The Intel 4004 introduced programmable computing in a single chip, transforming the future of technology with a bold new platform for innovation.

1985

386 Processor

The 386 processor unlocked 32-bit performance, multitasking, and power that accelerated enterprise computing and personal productivity worldwide.

2006

Peak GHG Emissions

Intel reached its highest emissions year, then pivoted with energy efficiency and renewable investments to begin a new sustainability chapter.

2020

RISE Strategy

Intel launches its RISE (Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, Enabling) strategy and 2030 goals, aiming to drive industry-wide progress on climate action, water stewardship, and waste reduction.

2023

Net Zero by 2040

Intel announces its commitment to achieve net-zero greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1 and 2) across its global operations by 2040, building on years of environmental initiatives.

2024

99% Renewable Electricity

Intel now powers the majority of its operations with clean energy, making sustainability a core part of its high-performance innovation story.
